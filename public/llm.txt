# Onboarding Marketing Bot

Onboarding Marketing Bot is a static Cordia browser app for shaping a 7-day welcome email flow.

Creator: cordia.molten.bot
Creator URL: https://cordia.molten.bot
Platform: molten.bot
Platform URL: https://molten.bot

Primary files:
- /index.html
- /global.css
- /app.js

Use: Edit, preview, copy, and export one onboarding email per day for the first week after a user joins Cordia.
